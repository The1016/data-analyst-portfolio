Case Study 1

Automated Sales Reporting System

Problem: A business receives monthly sales CSV files and needs a repeatable reporting workflow instead of manually cleaning and combining files every month.

Solution: Created a Python pipeline that loads monthly files, combines them, cleans the dataset, removes duplicates, standardizes dates/text fields, creates business columns, exports clean data, and connects the output to Power BI.

Result: A refreshable Power BI report covering executive sales, product/margin analysis, customer/region analysis, and data quality/refresh summary.

Tools: Python, Pandas, Power BI, DAX, Power Query, CSV.
