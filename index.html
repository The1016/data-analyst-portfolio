04 The approach

Good analysis starts
before the chart.

Understand the question. Trust the data.
Make the answer useful.

01

Ask the right question.

Understand the business goal, the available files, and the decision the report needs to support.

Context first
02

Give the data structure.

Clean dates and categories, check duplicates and missing values, and build consistent metrics.

Quality matters
03

Make the answer visible.

Build focused dashboards and explain the findings with clear, practical recommendations.

Decision ready
04
